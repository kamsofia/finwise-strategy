# The Mechanic · FinWise

> Module 3 · Retention & Engagement. The engagement mechanic that builds the habit loop, with rationale and a wireframe.

## The mechanic

**Mechanic:** Custom

- **Trigger:** A Monday 8:00 weekly cash summary email, switched on during onboarding. It shows cash, runway and their goal, with a button to open the check-in in the app.
- **Action:** Reviewing their own connected cash position (money in, money out, days of runway) and confirming or fixing the categorization, as a recurring weekly check-in
- **Reward:** The bar moves toward the goal they set. A line shows what moved it, for example “Two paid invoices added 3 days.” Crossing a tick shows one line: “You passed 45 days. Your cash now covers a month and a half.”
- **Investment:** They set their own goal, as days of runway or a cash amount. They send reminders to customers who owe them and fix categorizations. Both make next Monday’s numbers more accurate.

_____

## Rationale

This addresses value churn, specifically silent disengagement: owners keep paying but stop opening the product. The check-in gives a reason to come back every Monday. The bar measures their own goal, so it is something they would lose track of without the visits. A user about to churn would stay because FinWise shows progress toward their number and who still owes them money, which a spreadsheet doesn’t do. It does not address price churn or failed payments. Those need billing fixes, not gamification.
