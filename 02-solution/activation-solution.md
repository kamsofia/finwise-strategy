# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## Aha moment

The exact moment FinWise becomes “my money, finally clear” is when a new trial user connects their own bank account and, within seconds, sees their real cash position (money in, money out, and how many days of runway they have) already categorized, without having entered a single number by hand.

## Why this activates

This works because a trial user reaches their own cash position (runway, money in and out, already categorized) in about two taps and one session, with no data entry. The friction I removed was manual setup: the business profile, product tour, invoice setup, plan picker and hand-entered numbers, which is where most trial users stall before seeing any value. This matters because reaching Aha fast predicts retention. Users who see their own real numbers early are the ones who form a repeat habit (the weekly summary) and stay, which is what moves today's 2% trial-to-paid conversion and 40% one-year retention. To validate: compare week-one behavior of users retained past 90 days against churned users.
