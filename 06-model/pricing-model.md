# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, Value isn't proven yet: 98.0% of trials never convert (6,424 trials → 128 paid), and the rate stays flat at 1.87–2.08% in every one of the 13 months even when usage swings widely. Churned users also outnumber new paid users in every month, which fits the 40% one-year retention. Pricing isn't the constraint yet; value delivery is.

**Model:** Subscription, FinWise delivers value continuously (a weekly cash check-in on a connected bank), and retention is the main growth lever, so subscription fits. Freemium would add top-of-funnel scale, but scale isn't the problem: visits and trials barely correlate, and more paid acquisition no longer moves growth. A free tier would add serving cost and cannibalize the paid tier without fixing why trial users don't pay. The trade-off is that conversion stays capped by the trial, so I accept a slower top of funnel in exchange for protecting ARPU and fixing value delivery first.

_____

## Recommendation

Keep the subscription price and model unchanged and move the free/paid line at the end of the reverse trial. Users who connected a bank and reached the Aha screen keep their personal cash view, but sharing with an accountant or co-owner, and the weekly check-in, move to the paid plan. This targets new reverse-trial users who reach Aha in their first session. It is sequenced after the M5 share-step test shows invites happen at all.

**The pricing bet:** Is packaging matched to value? Validating that the free/paid line sits where users feel they need more.

_____

## Model signal

Targets my M4 leading indicator "% of trial users who invite their accountant, bookkeeper or co-owner within 7 days." The paywall sits where shared work exists, so users who invited someone feel the need to keep it, and conversions should cluster at that boundary instead of scattering. It also fits Stage 1, since it changes packaging, not price level.
